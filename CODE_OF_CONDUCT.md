# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Offer constructive feedback, assume
good intent, and make room for different experience levels and perspectives.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable in project
issues, pull requests, or other community spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, restrict participation, or
ban repeat or serious offenders. Enforcement should be proportionate and protect
the privacy of affected people.

To report a concern, request a private contact channel from a maintainer without
posting personal details publicly. Do not use vulnerability reports for ordinary
conduct disputes.
